What Every AMSO Conference Includes

💬

Invited Talks & Keynotes

International and Nigerian scholars present on the conference theme, followed by open discussion.

💻

Hands-On Workshops

Pre-conference training in Python, R, LaTeX, MATLAB/Maple and applied optimization software.

📄

Paper Presentation & Publication

Accepted abstracts are presented at the conference; full papers go through journal-based review for the IJMSOTA.

Submitting a paper or registering for the next conference?

Abstracts (max 350 words, MS Word/LaTeX + PDF) go to the secretariat. Registration and updates are announced through AMSO's channels.
