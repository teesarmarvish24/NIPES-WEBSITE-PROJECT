Publication

International Journal of Mathematics Sciences and Optimization: Theory and Applications

IJMSOTA is AMSO's peer-reviewed outlet for research in mathematical analysis, optimization, statistics and their applications, drawing heavily on papers presented at the annual ICAPTA/ICMSO conference.

📖

Scope

Pure and applied mathematics, fixed point theory, optimization theory and methods, numerical analysis, operations research, statistics, and mathematical modelling of real-world (industrial, financial, biological and computational) problems.

✎

Route to Publication

Abstracts are first submitted for conference presentation (max 350 words, MS Word or LaTeX with PDF). Following presentation and feedback, full papers are submitted for journal-based review and, on acceptance, published in IJMSOTA.
