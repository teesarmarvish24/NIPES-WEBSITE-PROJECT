Join the AMSO Community

Membership connects you with researchers, students and practitioners in mathematical analysis, optimization, statistics and data science — with reduced conference fees and access to AMSO support.

Why Join

Membership Benefits

🎓

Discounted Registration

Students and registered members receive a 50% discount on conference and workshop fees.

🤝

Network & Collaborate

Connect with researchers from Nigeria, Benin Republic and the wider international mathematics community.

💰

Research Support

Registered AMSO members can apply for support toward research and conference participation.

📱

Hands-On Training

Priority access to workshop tracks in Python, R, LaTeX and applied optimization software.

📄

Publish in IJMAO

A clear pathway from conference paper presentation to journal-reviewed publication.

💬

Stay Informed

Direct updates on calls for papers, registration deadlines and upcoming conference themes.

How to Join

Three Simple Steps

1

Register Your Interest

Contact the AMSO secretariat or complete the registration form linked with the current conference call for papers.

2

Confirm Payment

Pay the applicable registration fee; students should provide valid ID for the discount rate.

3

Get Involved

Attend the annual conference, join a workshop track, and consider submitting a paper to IJMAO.
