Who We Are

A Community Built on Research & Collaboration

AMSO grew out of the Mathematical Analysis and Optimization Research Group (MANORG) at the University of Lagos, and now works in close partnership with the Mathematical Analysis and Applications Research Group (MAARG) and national and international mathematics institutes.

∑

AMSO

The Association of Mathematical Sciences and Optimization is the professional body coordinating conferences, membership, publications and outreach across the field.

◯

MANORG & MAARG

The Mathematical Analysis and Optimization Research Group and the Mathematical Analysis and Applications Research Group, based in the Department of Mathematics, University of Lagos, drive the research agenda behind AMSO's programmes.

🌐

Partner Institutions

AMSO collaborates with the National Mathematical Centre (Abuja), the Institut de Mathématique et de Sciences Physiques (Benin), and the International Mathematical Union.

Flagship Programme

ICAPTA / ICMSO Conference Series

Held annually since the mid-2010s, the conference has grown from a UNILAG-based workshop into an international gathering — hosted with the National Mathematical Centre, Abuja and, most recently, IMSP Benin.

Publication

International Journal of Mathematics Sciences and Optimization: Theory and Applications

IJMSOTA publishes reviewed full papers arising from AMSO conferences and open submissions, covering pure and applied mathematics, optimization, and statistics.
